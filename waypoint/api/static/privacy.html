Waypoint

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Waypoint is a student project built and run by Ali Ismail. It is not an official University of Toronto service, and it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the University.

The short version

  • We keep your Google account ID so your conversations stay yours. We don't store your name or email address.
  • Your questions and Waypoint's answers are stored so a conversation can continue. Emails, phone numbers and student numbers are removed first.
  • Your questions are sent to Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI to produce answers.
  • Logs and traces hold timings, costs and a pseudonymous ID, not the text of your questions.
  • Waypoint never accesses ACORN, transcripts, grades or any university account.
  • Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
  • To have your data deleted, email alii.ekramy.ismail@gmail.com.

Signing in with Google

Waypoint uses Google's "Sign in with Google" button. When you sign in, Google gives your browser a signed token that contains your Google account ID, name, email address, whether the email is verified, and profile picture link. Your browser sends the token with each question, and Waypoint's server checks it with Google's public keys.

From that token, Waypoint:

  • keeps your Google account ID, a number that identifies your account, to keep your conversations separate from everyone else's;
  • uses but doesn't store your email address, only to check whether you are the site's operator;
  • shows your first name in the page header. The name stays in your browser.

Your profile picture isn't used. The token lives only in the open page and is gone when you close it. Waypoint sets no cookies of its own. Google's sign-in button may set its own cookie (for example, to remember that you dismissed the sign-in prompt), and Google's privacy policy covers that.

Your conversations

Conversations are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, in the United States (us-central1), under your Google account ID. A conversation record holds:

  • your questions, after redaction (see below);
  • Waypoint's answers;
  • what Waypoint looked up to answer: calendar passages, course information, and any course plans it drafted and checked for you.

There is no separate "saved plans" feature. A plan exists only as part of the conversation where Waypoint drafted it.

How long: conversations don't expire automatically yet. They are kept until you ask for them to be deleted, or until the project is shut down.

Ratings, advisor clicks and usage records

To understand whether Waypoint helps, it records a small usage event in Firestore:

  • Each question: response time, cost, and whether the question was off-topic.
  • Thumbs up or down: your rating of an answer.
  • "Ask an advisor": that you clicked it. The link opens UTM's advising website, and Waypoint sends nothing to advisors.

Each event also holds a pseudonymous ID (a one-way hash of your Google account ID), the conversation ID and the answer ID. Events never contain the text of your questions or answers, and they are kept on the same terms as conversations. The operator can see totals, such as the number of users and the thumbs-up rate, not individual conversations.

AI processing: Vertex AI Gemini

To answer, Waypoint sends Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI your question (after redaction), the earlier turns of the same conversation, and the calendar passages and course data it looked up. Search queries based on your question are also turned into embeddings, which are numeric representations used to find relevant calendar passages, with Google's embedding model. Requests go through Google's global endpoint, so they may be processed outside Canada. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google doesn't use this data to train its models.

Logs and traces

  • Cloud Logging (Google Cloud). Waypoint writes one line per question with the pseudonymous ID, the conversation ID and numbers: timings, tokens, cost, and how many sources were cited. If something was redacted, the line says only which kind (for example, "email"), never the value. Google Cloud Run also keeps standard request logs: your IP address, browser type, time and the page requested. Logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Cloud Trace (Google Cloud) and Langfuse. Waypoint sends performance traces: how long each step took, token counts, cost, which tools were used, the pseudonymous ID and the conversation ID. Langfuse is a third-party service for monitoring AI applications. Traces don't include the text of your questions or answers; this is enforced in Waypoint's code and checked by its automated tests. Cloud Trace keeps traces for 30 days.

Personal information redaction

Before your question reaches the AI model, the conversation store or the traces, Waypoint replaces email addresses, phone numbers and 9–10 digit numbers (such as student numbers) with a placeholder like "[redacted email]". It does the same to answers. This works by matching patterns, so it can't catch everything. Names, addresses, grades and anything else you type are not removed. Please don't enter sensitive personal information.

What Waypoint never does

  • It never signs in to or reads ACORN, Quercus, transcripts, grades or any University of Toronto system. Its course information comes from the public Academic Calendar.
  • It never sells your data or shares it for advertising, and the site has no ads, ad trackers or third-party analytics.

Who can access your data

The project's developer, as the owner of the Google Cloud project, and the service providers named above: Google Cloud (Firestore, Vertex AI, Cloud Logging, Cloud Trace) and Langfuse, each acting on Waypoint's behalf. Data is disclosed to anyone else only if the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Email alii.ekramy.ismail@gmail.com from the Google account you used with Waypoint. Waypoint doesn't store email addresses, so the reply will include a simple step to confirm which account is yours. Then your conversations and usage records are deleted. Logs and traces can't be linked to you by name and expire on the schedules above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change too. Material changes will be noted on the main page.
